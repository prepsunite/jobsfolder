## 🤖 Recommended 1-Click Publishing Bot Architecture (Phase 2 & 3)

Now that we have verified feeds yielding **2,300+ leads**, here is the 1-click publishing workflow:

1. **Scraping Pipeline (Cron or On-Demand):** Automatically pulls from these verified sources daily.
2. **Staging / Review Table in Supabase (`job_leads_staging`):**
   - Stores freshly scraped leads with `status = 'PENDING'`.
   - Stores company, title, apply link, salary, location, batch, source, and scraped date.
3. **Admin Quick-Review Interface (or Telegram / Discord Bot):**
   - In our Prepunite Admin portal or via a private notification channel, you view cards of new leads.
   - Click **Approve (1-Click Publish)**: Instantly inserts into public `job_listings` table with active visibility.
   - Click **Reject**: Marks as dismissed or duplicates.
4. **Duplicate Prevention:** Unique constraint on `company_name + title + batch` ensures zero duplicate clutter.

*Full machine-readable dataset saved at `scripts/output/job_leads_raw.json`.*
